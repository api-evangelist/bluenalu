# BlueNalu

> BlueNalu, Inc. is a San Diego, California cellular aquaculture company founded in 2017 that produces cell-cultivated seafood — real seafood grown directly from fish cells rather than harvested from the ocean — beginning with cell-cultivated bluefin tuna toro for premium foodservice operators. BlueNalu is a food and biotechnology producer and publishes no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable contract.
